# Community conduct

Be respectful, patient, and specific. This project serves beginners, so no one
should be mocked for unfamiliarity with terminals, authentication, networking,
or security.

Do not request, post, or quote anyone's passwords, tokens, private keys, login
files, personal paths, chat content, or unredacted logs. If sensitive material
appears, stop the conversation and direct the person to rotate or revoke it.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, and
unsafe pressure to bypass security controls are not accepted. Maintainers may
edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and participation that violate these
rules.

Report security problems through `SECURITY.md`. Report conduct concerns to the
repository owner through GitHub's private contact mechanisms.
